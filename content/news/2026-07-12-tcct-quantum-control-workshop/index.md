Zhiyu Jiang gave a talk at the 13th TCCT Quantum Control Workshop held in Shenyang.

The talk, entitled *"Isometrization of Tensor Network States via Gauge Propagation"*, presented recent work by Zhiyu Jiang and Hiroshi Ueda on approximate isometrization of tensor-network states using gauge propagation.

This work introduces a practical framework for converting generic tensor-network states into approximately isometric forms with a prescribed orthogonality center. The method is motivated by tensor-network representations of quantum many-body states and aims to provide useful building blocks for isometric tensor-network algorithms.

The presentation was based on the recent arXiv preprint:

[Isometrization of Tensor Network States via Gauge Propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22816)
