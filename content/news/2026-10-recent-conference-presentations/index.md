Members of the Ueda Group recently presented their research at several international and domestic conferences and workshops.

The presentations covered recent developments in tensor-network isometrization, variational quantum algorithms, and quantum-classical approaches to entanglement-spectrum estimation.

## Zhiyu Jiang

### SQAI-NCTS Joint Workshop 2026
**Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Venue:** Keio University, Yokohama, Japan  
**Presentation:** Oral  

**Title:** *"Isometrization of Tensor Network States via Gauge Propagation"*

Zhiyu Jiang presented recent work on the isometrization of tensor-network states using gauge propagation.

### 22nd Quantum Spin Systems Workshop
**Date:** August 29, 2026  
**Venue:** Tsuna Heights, Awaji Island, Japan  
**Presentation:** Oral  

**Title:** *"Isometrization of Tensor Network States via Gauge Propagation"*

Zhiyu Jiang presented the group's recent work on gauge-propagation-based isometrization of tensor-network states.

### 81st Annual Meeting of the Physical Society of Japan
**Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Venue:** Komaba Campus, The University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan  
**Presentation:** Oral  

**Presenters:** Zhiyu Jiang and Hiroshi Ueda  

**Title:** *"Isometrization of Tensor Network States via Gauge Propagation"*

The presentation reported recent progress on converting generic tensor-network states into approximately isometric forms through gauge propagation.

## Shohei Miyakoshi

### AQIS 2026
**Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Venue:** KAIST, Daejeon, Korea  
**Presentation:** Poster  

**Authors:** S. Miyakoshi, T. Sugimoto, T. Shirakawa, S. Yunoki, and H. Ueda  

**Title:** *"A Concurrent Quantum-Classical Framework for Entanglement Spectrum Estimation via Orthogonality Correction"*

The poster presented a quantum-classical framework for estimating entanglement spectra using variational quantum methods with classical orthogonality correction.

### TQC 2026
**Date:** August 31, 2026  
**Venue:** Delta Hotels by Marriott Sherbrooke Conference Center, Sherbrooke, Canada  
**Presentation:** Poster  

**Authors:** S. Miyakoshi, T. Sugimoto, T. Shirakawa, S. Yunoki, and H. Ueda  

**Title:** *"A Concurrent Hybrid Framework for Variational Quantum SVD via Classical Orthogonality Correction"*

The presentation introduced a hybrid framework for variational quantum singular-value decomposition incorporating classical orthogonality correction.

### 81st Annual Meeting of the Physical Society of Japan
**Date:** September 16, 2026  
**Venue:** Komaba Campus, The University of Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan  
**Presentation:** Oral  

**Authors:** Shohei Miyakoshi, Takanori Sugimoto, Tomonori Shirakawa, Seiji Yunoki, and Hiroshi Ueda  

**Title:** *"Quantum-Classical Hybrid Implementation of Entanglement Spectrum Estimation Based on Variational Quantum Singular Value Decomposition"*

The talk presented a quantum-classical hybrid implementation of entanglement-spectrum estimation based on variational quantum singular-value decomposition.

These presentations provided valuable opportunities to discuss our recent work with researchers in tensor networks, quantum many-body physics, and quantum information science.
